Route Student Progress Report

A Chrome Extension for teachers and training centers that generates and sends professional student progress reports straight from Google Sheets, using the instructor's own Gmail account.

What this app isAbout the Extension

Route Student Progress Report is a Chrome Extension built for instructors, tutoring centers, and educational organizations who track student performance in Google Sheets and need a fast way to turn that data into individual, professional-looking progress reports sent by email.


After you install the extension and open a spreadsheet that contains your student data, the extension reads the rows you select, builds a formatted HTML report for each student, and lets you preview it before anything is sent.


When you choose to send, the report is emailed directly from your own Gmail account through Google's official Gmail API. The extension does not run its own mail server and does not relay, copy, or store your emails or spreadsheet data anywhere.

FunctionalityMain Features

Google Sheets Integration

Step by stepHow It Works

  1. Open the Google Sheets spreadsheet that contains your student data.
  2. Launch the Route Student Progress Report extension from your browser toolbar.
  3. Sign in with your Google Account and review the permissions requested.
  4. Select which students should receive a report.
  5. Preview the generated HTML email for accuracy.
  6. Send the reports — each one goes out from your own Gmail account.

Why we askGoogle Permissions & How Your Data Is Used

Google Sheets — read-only access

Used only to retrieve the student names, email addresses, grades, attendance, and assignment details that already exist in the spreadsheet you open, so the extension can build the report content. The extension never edits, deletes, or writes anything back to your spreadsheet.

Gmail — send-only access

Used only to send the report email you have previewed and explicitly chosen to send, from your own Gmail account. The extension cannot read your inbox, and never sends anything automatically or without your action.

No student information, spreadsheet contents, Gmail messages, or OAuth tokens are ever stored on servers we operate. All processing happens locally in the browser, and every request goes directly to Google's official Sheets and Gmail APIs. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

TrustPrivacy & Security

  • No student information is stored by us.
  • No Gmail messages are stored by us.
  • No spreadsheets are copied or cached.
  • No third-party servers process your data — only Google's own APIs are used.
  • Authentication is handled entirely by Google OAuth.
  • You may revoke access at any time from your Google Account.
